Oxynet

This demo has retired. Oxynet now lives at app.oxynet.net.

Drop a cardiopulmonary exercise test export there. No account is needed. Oxynet reads the file, checks the signal, and finds both ventilatory thresholds with the current models, along with substrate use and the rest of the analysis.

Go to app.oxynet.net You will be taken there in a few seconds. For assistants and code, see www.oxynet.net/developers.
